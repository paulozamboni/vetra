Realistic skin texture and hands, premium professional travel photography. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI. This is an illustrative campaign with fictional adults, not an actual influencer. Output 1536x1024 or wider landscape.

Upper quarter uncluttered for quotation form. No text, no logos, no watermark, no user interface. Not a depiction of a real influencer. Output landscape.
