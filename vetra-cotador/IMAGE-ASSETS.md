# Imagens de campanha

Geradas com a ferramenta ImageGen integrada. As pessoas são personagens fictícios, não o influenciador futuro.

- `public/images/banner-couple.png`: casal em viagem costeira, composição horizontal, pessoas à direita e espaço livre para o cotador e os textos.
- `public/images/banner-traveler.png`: viajante em uma cidade, composição horizontal, personagem à direita e espaço para os textos à esquerda.

## Prompts utilizados

Casal: Create a premium travel insurance website banner photo, horizontal wide 16:9. Candid editorial photography of a joyful Brazilian couple in their early thirties exploring a beautiful coastal European town on vacation, unbranded daypacks, natural authentic expressions, fashionable ordinary travel clothes, afternoon sunshine, ocean and sunlit architecture. Composition: both people in the RIGHT THIRD and lower half, waist-up, spacious softly detailed ocean and sky on LEFT HALF for a dark navy overlay and page headline, upper quarter mostly clear scenery for an overlaid quotation form. Realistic skin texture and hands, premium professional travel photography. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI. This is an illustrative campaign with fictional adults, not an actual influencer. Output 1536x1024 or wider landscape.

Viajante: Premium travel brand website banner photograph, horizontal landscape 16:9. A fictional Brazilian woman traveler around thirty with curly dark hair enjoying a city break with an unbranded small rolling suitcase, looking at the architecture with an authentic relaxed smile, in a sunlit European street with elegant buildings and a cafe in background. Cinematic editorial photography, rich natural blue and green accents, warm afternoon daylight, realistic human features and hands. Subject placed in the RIGHT THIRD, waist-up or three-quarter, LEFT HALF has quiet softly blurred architecture to support headline under dark navy overlay. Upper quarter uncluttered for quotation form. No text, no logos, no watermark, no user interface. Not a depiction of a real influencer. Output landscape.
